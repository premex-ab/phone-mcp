Phone MCP turns your Android phone into a secure MCP server that AI assistants like Claude can connect to. Ask your AI to find a photo, read a downloaded PDF, check a sensor, snap a picture or look up a contact — while every capability stays under your control, on your device.

The app contains no AI of its own. You connect the assistant you already use — Claude Code, Claude Desktop, or any client that speaks the Model Context Protocol.

Remote access

Reach your phone from anywhere through phonemcp.ai. Pair your AI client: run one command, type the pairing code the app shows you, done. Paired clients reconnect automatically.

• Works from any network — no port forwarding, no VPN, no account creation
• OAuth 2.1 authorization with single-use pairing codes
• Your phone only ever makes outbound connections
• The relay never sees your phone's local credentials
• See and revoke every paired AI client in the app
• Free 30-day remote trial, then an optional monthly or yearly subscription
• Paid plans renew automatically until canceled in Google Play; local Wi-Fi remains free

Local network

On your own Wi-Fi, your AI connects directly to the phone. No cloud, no subscription, no limits. This will always be free.

On-device assistants (experimental)

On Android 16 and newer, assistants with AppFunctions access can use enabled Phone MCP tools directly — no MCP server connection needed. Search contacts, take a photo, get location and sensor snapshots, browse files or prepare an SMS for review. The same tool switches control MCP and assistant access, while normal Android permissions continue to apply. Assistant support is currently limited during Android's experimental preview and depends on your device, assistant and account.

Try a useful first task

• Enable Sensors and ask: “Use phone_sensor. Which sensors are available, and what does one reading mean?”
• Enable Files, choose a shared folder, then ask your AI to list the documents before opening one.
• Enable Camera and ask an image-capable AI to inspect a photo of the object you are working on.

The setup guide confirms a successful remote tool response and helps with connection problems. Your AI client interprets the results; Phone MCP provides the tools.

What your AI can do

• Files — browse and read photos, downloads and documents
• Camera — take a photo and hand it straight to the model
• Sensors — accelerometer, gyroscope, barometer, light and more
• Location — where the phone is right now
• Contacts — look up people
• SMS — compose messages (with your confirmation)
• Extend it — third-party apps can expose their own MCP tools

Private by design

• Every tool is off until you enable it — sensitive ones require explicit consent
• Bearer-token authentication locally, OAuth 2.1 remotely
• Turn remote access off at any time; the tunnel closes immediately
• No ads and no sale of your data
• Service metrics and Firebase diagnostics are described in our privacy policy; tool contents are not saved as analytics

Open source
