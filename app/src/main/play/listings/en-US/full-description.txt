📱 Your phone, as tools for your AI

Phone MCP turns your Android phone into a secure MCP server that AI assistants like Claude can connect to. Ask your AI to find a photo, read a downloaded PDF, check a sensor, snap a picture or look up a contact — while every capability stays under your control, on your device.

The app contains no AI of its own. You connect the assistant you already use — Claude Code, Claude Desktop, or any client that speaks the Model Context Protocol.

🌍 REMOTE ACCESS — NEW
Reach your phone from anywhere through phonemcp.ai. Pair a new AI client in under a minute: run one command, type the pairing code the app shows you, done. Paired clients reconnect automatically.

• Works from any network — no port forwarding, no VPN, no account creation
• OAuth 2.1 authorization with single-use pairing codes
• Your phone only ever makes outbound connections
• The relay never sees your phone's local credentials
• See and revoke every paired AI client in the app
• Free 30-day trial, then a small monthly or yearly subscription

🏠 LOCAL NETWORK — FREE FOREVER
On your own Wi-Fi, your AI connects directly to the phone. No cloud, no subscription, no limits. This will always be free.

🧰 WHAT YOUR AI CAN DO
• Files — browse and read photos, downloads and documents
• Camera — take a photo and hand it straight to the model
• Sensors — accelerometer, gyroscope, barometer, light and more
• Location — where the phone is right now
• Contacts — look up people
• SMS — compose messages (with your confirmation)
• Extend it — third-party apps can expose their own MCP tools

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Every tool is OFF until you enable it — sensitive ones require explicit consent
• Bearer-token authentication locally, OAuth 2.1 remotely
• Turn remote access off at any time; the tunnel closes immediately
• We run no ads and no trackers, and never sell data
